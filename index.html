About This System

Indoor air and ventilation management system manages indoor air quality in real time and controls ventilation to maintain a safe and comfortable environment. The system reads CO₂, PM2.5, temperature, and humidity levels. When air quality drops, the fan can automatically turn on to improve ventilation.
